== Legal and Reliance Limitations

=== Nature of this Standard

ZACS is a voluntary contribution-governance standard. It defines conformance
requirements for Projects that choose to adopt it.

Publication of this Standard does not compel any person or Project to adopt it.
Reference to ZACS, without a conforming Adoption Notice and implementation,
does not establish ZACS conformance.

This Standard does not determine whether an Adoption Notice, Project Policy,
Attestation, or contribution workflow forms a contract. That question depends
on the applicable facts, terms, and law.

=== No legal or professional advice

This Standard and its accompanying documentation provide general standards
information. They do not provide legal, regulatory, security, privacy,
employment, tax, accounting, or other professional advice and do not create an
attorney-client or other professional relationship.

A Project or Contributor remains responsible for obtaining qualified advice
appropriate to its jurisdiction, risks, obligations, and intended use.

ZACS conformance MUST NOT be represented as a substitute for legal review,
license review, security review, privacy assessment, regulatory analysis, or
other professional due diligence.

=== No determination of legal status

ZACS does not determine:

* whether a human, organization, or automated system is an author or inventor;
* whether material is protected by copyright, patent, trademark, trade-secret,
  database, publicity, privacy, contract, or other rights;
* ownership, work-made-for-hire status, agency, authorization, or employment
  status;
* copyrightability, patentability, originality, substantial similarity,
  infringement, fair use, fair dealing, or another exception or limitation;
* whether a license is valid, enforceable, compatible, or sufficient for a
  particular use;
* the provenance, contents, or legal status of model training data; or
* whether generated or transformed material is free of third-party rights.

Terms such as `Contributor`, `Human Review`, `Material Assistance`, and
`No Material Assistance` have the meanings assigned by this Standard. They do
not establish a legal conclusion outside ZACS.

=== No grant or transfer of Contribution rights

Adoption of ZACS and completion of a ZACS Attestation do not grant, transfer,
assign, sublicense, or waive copyright, patent, trademark, trade-secret, or
other rights in a Contribution.

Rights applicable to a Contribution arise, if at all, through the Project's
license, contribution terms, CLA, DCO process, employment or contractor terms,
or another applicable agreement or legal rule.

A Project MUST keep any required license or rights-acquisition process legally
distinct from ZACS and MUST NOT rely on ZACS as the source of that grant. The
Project MUST NOT represent the ZACS Attestation as a CLA, copyright assignment,
patent license, or DCO. A legally distinct instrument MAY be collected through
the same workflow when its terms and effect remain separately identifiable.

=== Relationship to the Developer Certificate of Origin

ZACS is designed to complement the unmodified Developer Certificate of Origin
(DCO). It does not amend, replace, interpret, or expand the DCO.

A Project MAY collect a ZACS Attestation and DCO sign-off through the same
workflow, but each instrument MUST remain identifiable and the Project MUST NOT
represent modified DCO wording as the unmodified DCO.

Satisfying ZACS does not establish DCO compliance, and satisfying the DCO does
not establish ZACS compliance.

=== Patents

ZACS does not require a Contributor, Project, implementer, or standards
participant to grant a patent license merely by discussing, proposing,
adopting, implementing, or contributing to this Standard.

No representation is made that practicing ZACS or implementing related tooling
is free of patent claims. Patent rights and obligations, if any, depend on
separate licenses, policies, agreements, and applicable law.

A future patent policy applies only according to its own terms and MUST NOT be
treated as retroactively granted by this edition of ZACS unless the affected
rights holder expressly agrees.

=== Attestations and Evidence

An Attestation is a statement by an identifiable human. Disclosure,
Documentation, review, testing, demonstration, and other Verification records
are forms of Evidence.

No such record independently guarantees:

* the identity, authority, or legal capacity of a person;
* authorship, ownership, originality, or lawful provenance;
* accuracy or completeness of a Disclosure or Attestation;
* license compatibility or absence of infringement;
* correctness, security, privacy, accessibility, or maintainability; or
* fitness for a particular purpose.

Projects remain responsible for deciding what additional diligence is
appropriate. Contributors remain responsible for the truth and completeness of
their statements to the extent required by applicable Project terms and law.

=== Automated detection and technical limitations

No automated authorship detector, similarity detector, provenance tool,
license scanner, security scanner, model-identification system, or comparable
technology is assumed to be conclusive under ZACS.

Automated tools may produce false positives, false negatives, incomplete
results, or results that require contextual interpretation. A Project MUST
follow the Human Review and enforcement requirements of its adopted profiles
before relying on material automated results for a ZACS determination.

ZACS does not warrant the availability, accuracy, interoperability, or
continued behavior of any third-party system, service, model, registry, URL, or
tool referenced by a Project.

=== Privacy, confidentiality, and protected information

ZACS does not authorize collection, use, disclosure, retention, or transfer of
personal data, confidential information, credentials, trade secrets, privileged
material, or protected security information.

Nothing in ZACS authorizes noncompliance with applicable law or separate duties
governing such material. Projects and Contributors remain independently
responsible for those obligations. A Project SHOULD design Disclosure,
Documentation, Verification, and enforcement procedures to collect no more
protected information than reasonably necessary.

When public disclosure is unsafe, unlawful, or prohibited, a Project MAY use a
protected mechanism consistent with the adopted profile. Use of a protected
mechanism does not permit omission of the required non-sensitive substance.

=== Accessibility, employment, and organizational policy

ZACS does not define an employment qualification, professional credential, or
medical, disability, or educational assessment.

Projects remain responsible for applicable accessibility,
anti-discrimination, labor, employment, contractor, collective-bargaining, and
accommodation obligations.

An organization MAY impose internal requirements stricter than ZACS, but it
MUST distinguish those requirements from the unmodified Standard when making a
ZACS conformance claim.

=== Regulatory and contractual compliance

ZACS conformance does not establish compliance with any statute, regulation,
court order, government requirement, procurement rule, industry code,
contract, insurance condition, or platform policy.

Conversely, compliance with another legal, contractual, regulatory, or policy
requirement does not establish ZACS conformance.

A party subject to multiple requirements remains responsible for satisfying
each applicable requirement. When requirements conflict, the party SHOULD seek
qualified advice and MUST NOT conceal the unresolved conflict in a ZACS
conformance claim.

=== No endorsement of third parties

Reference to a person, organization, Project, product, service, license,
standard, model, tool, or publication is for identification or explanation
unless expressly stated otherwise. A reference does not imply sponsorship,
endorsement, certification, approval, affiliation, or warranty.

Adoption of ZACS by a Project does not imply endorsement of that Project by the
ZACS steward, and participation in ZACS development does not imply endorsement
of every ZACS provision or adopter.

=== Mandatory law and jurisdiction

ZACS does not select a governing law, court, arbitral forum, or jurisdiction
for a Project, Contributor, Contribution, or adoption.

binding legal order. A requirement that cannot lawfully be applied in a
particular context does not authorize an inaccurate or unqualified conformance
claim.

Rights and obligations that cannot lawfully be excluded, limited, waived, or
transferred remain unaffected to the extent required by applicable law.
