== Verification Profiles

=== Purpose and selection

Verification Profiles define progressively stronger Project procedures for
obtaining Evidence of Contributor accountability, review, and understanding.

A conforming Project MUST select exactly one Verification Profile. The selected
profile incorporates every lower-numbered Verification Profile, subject only
to an express replacement stated in this section.

[cols="1,2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Profile |Name |Core requirement

|V0
|Attested
|The Project obtains and preserves an Attestation from the identifiable human
Contributor.

|V1
|Reviewed
|An independent human reviewer evaluates the Contribution, Attestation, and
required provenance records.

|V2
|Demonstrated
|The Contributor demonstrates relevant understanding without Contemporaneous
Generative Assistance.
|===

=== Common verification requirements

The Project MUST publish a Verification Procedure sufficient to implement its
selected Verification Profile. The procedure MUST identify:

. when Verification occurs;
. who may perform it;
. what material and records are reviewed;
. the possible outcomes;
. how deficiencies may be corrected or reconsidered; and
. what Evidence the Project preserves.

Verification MUST be applied to Covered Contributions according to objective
criteria published by the Project. A Project MAY vary the depth of a procedure
according to Contribution size, risk, subject matter, or Contributor history
when the Project Policy describes the basis for doing so.

A Project MUST NOT claim that Automated Review alone satisfies a requirement
for Human Review or demonstrated human understanding.

The Project SHOULD conduct Verification in a manner proportionate to the
Contribution and SHOULD avoid requesting confidential information, credentials,
personal data, or protected security information that is not necessary for the
procedure.

Verification supplies Evidence for a conformance decision. It does not prove
that an Attestation is true or that a Contribution is lawful, original,
correct, or secure.

=== V0 -- Attested

V0 establishes the minimum Verification requirements for every ZACS adoption.

Before accepting a Covered Contribution, the Project MUST obtain the
Attestation required by this Standard from the identifiable human Contributor.

The Attestation MUST:

. be an affirmative act rather than an assumption based only on submission;
. be attributable to the Contributor;
. identify or be reliably associated with the Covered Contribution;
. identify the applicable Adoption Identifier or a Project Policy that states
  it;
. contain or incorporate the assurances required by the selected Provenance
  Profile; and
. be preserved with the Contribution or its review record.

A checkbox, signed statement, commit trailer, pull-request declaration, or
other recorded mechanism MAY satisfy V0 when it meets every requirement above.

Silence, a preselected control, or a statement made solely by an automated
system MUST NOT constitute the Contributor's Attestation.

The Project MUST provide a reasonably accessible method for correcting or
withdrawing an Attestation. Correction does not erase the prior record when
preservation is necessary to understand the Contribution's review history.

V0 does not require the Project to independently confirm every fact asserted in
the Attestation. It requires an attributable, affirmative, and preserved human
Attestation.

=== V1 -- Reviewed

V1 incorporates V0 and requires independent Human Review.

Before accepting a Covered Contribution, at least one identifiable human other
than the Contributor MUST perform the required Human Review. The reviewer MUST
not have prepared the Contribution jointly in a manner that would prevent an
independent evaluation of the Contributor's Attestation and provenance records.

The Project Policy MUST identify how reviewer independence and material
conflicts of interest are handled.

The reviewer MUST:

. examine the complete Covered Contribution;
. examine the Contributor's Attestation;
. examine each Disclosure and item of Documentation required by the selected
  Provenance Profile;
. evaluate whether the records are complete and internally consistent;
. consider visible indications of undisclosed Material Assistance, Known
  Source Material, or incompatible provenance;
. evaluate whether the Contributor's described review and validation are
  reasonably appropriate to the Contribution; and
. record a review outcome and any unresolved material concern.

The reviewer MAY use Automated Review, search tools, provenance tools, license
scanners, testing systems, and other aids. The reviewer remains responsible for
the Human Review and MUST personally evaluate material results on which the
review outcome relies.

When the reviewer identifies a material omission, inconsistency, or concern,
the Project MUST obtain clarification or corrective action before acceptance.
The Project MUST NOT treat an unresolved material concern as satisfying V1.

V1 does not require a reviewer to reproduce every test, independently prove
ownership, or guarantee the Contribution. The depth of review MUST nevertheless
be reasonable for the size, complexity, risk, and nature of the Contribution.

The Project MUST preserve:

. the identity or durable Project identity of the reviewer;
. the review outcome;
. the material records reviewed;
. material questions, concerns, and resolutions; and
. the date or objectively determinable review event.

=== V2 -- Demonstrated

V2 incorporates V0 and V1 and requires the Contributor to demonstrate relevant
understanding to an independent human reviewer.

The demonstration MUST occur before acceptance and MUST be sufficient for the
reviewer to reasonably evaluate whether the Contributor understands the
Covered Contribution to the degree required by P0.

The Verification Procedure MUST require questions, explanations, modification
tasks, debugging tasks, or another reasonable method addressing material
aspects of the Contribution. As applicable, the procedure MUST address:

. the Contribution's purpose and methodology;
. material design or implementation decisions;
. behavior, operation, and limitations;
. provenance and Known Source Material;
. the role of disclosed Material Assistance;
. review and validation performed;
. material correctness, security, privacy, or licensing risks; and
. how the Contributor would maintain, revise, or correct the Contribution.

The procedure need not address every listed subject when a subject is not
applicable or material to the Contribution. The reviewer MUST select enough
relevant subjects to support the review outcome.

The Contributor MUST respond from their own understanding and MUST NOT use
Contemporaneous Generative Assistance while preparing or providing a response
that is part of the demonstration.

The Contributor MAY use ordinary accessibility tools, non-generative reference
materials, and conventional non-generative translation tools. The Project MUST
provide a reasonable accessible alternative when the default procedure would
prevent a Contributor with a disability or communication limitation from
demonstrating the same relevant understanding.

The demonstration MAY occur through asynchronous text, synchronous text,
voice, video, screen sharing, an interactive development environment, or
another method that permits meaningful evaluation. A Project MUST NOT require
voice or video when another method can provide materially equivalent Evidence.

The procedure MUST evaluate understanding of the Contribution rather than
unrelated memory, accent, language fluency, speed, personality, educational
credentials, or performance under unnecessary pressure.

The reviewer MAY ask reasonable follow-up questions and MAY request a focused
explanation or modification when an initial response is incomplete. The
reviewer MUST NOT demand unnecessary disclosure of confidential information,
credentials, personal data, protected security information, or verbatim AGS
conversation logs.

The reviewer MUST record whether the demonstration was satisfactory and MUST
record enough information to identify the subjects evaluated and the basis for
the outcome. The Project MAY preserve a written summary instead of an audio or
video recording.

A Contributor who does not demonstrate sufficient understanding MAY revise the
Contribution, improve their understanding, and repeat the procedure unless the
Project Policy provides a reasonable basis for another outcome. The Covered
Contribution MUST NOT be accepted under V2 until the demonstration is
satisfactory.

An employer, organization, or other representative MUST NOT substitute another
person's understanding for that of the Contributor making the Attestation.

=== Suspected procedural assistance or misrepresentation

When a reviewer has a reasonable, articulable basis to suspect prohibited
Contemporaneous Generative Assistance or material misrepresentation, the
reviewer MAY request clarification or repeat relevant portions of the
Verification Procedure through another accessible method.

Suspicion MUST NOT be based solely on writing style, disability, use of an
accessibility tool, language proficiency, response speed, or disagreement with
the reviewer.

The Project SHOULD distinguish a good-faith mistake or misunderstanding from
intentional concealment. Any corrective or enforcement action MUST follow the
published Project Policy and SHOULD be proportionate to the available Evidence.

=== Profile comparison

[cols="3,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Requirement |V0 |V1 |V2

|Affirmative human Attestation
|Yes |Yes |Yes

|Attestation preserved
|Yes |Yes |Yes

|Independent Human Review
|No |Yes |Yes

|Review of required provenance records
|No |Yes |Yes

|Contributor demonstrates understanding
|No |No |Yes

|Contemporaneous Generative Assistance prohibited during demonstration
|Not applicable |Not applicable |Yes

|Accessible equivalent procedure required
|General accessibility |General accessibility |Yes
|===

"No" in this table means the identified requirement is not imposed by that
profile. It does not prohibit a Project from adopting a stricter compatible
requirement.
