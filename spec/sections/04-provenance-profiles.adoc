== Provenance Profiles

=== Purpose and selection

Provenance Profiles define progressively stronger requirements for human
accountability, disclosure, documentation, control, and restriction of AGS use
in covered Contributions.

A conforming Project MUST select exactly one Provenance Profile. The selected
profile incorporates every lower-numbered Provenance Profile, subject only to
an express replacement stated in this section.

[cols="1,2,5",options="header"]
|===
|Profile |Name |Core requirement

|P0
|Accountable
|An identifiable human reviews, understands, and accepts responsibility for
the Contribution.

|P1
|Disclosed
|Material Assistance is disclosed.

|P2
|Documented
|Material Assistance and its review are documented.

|P3
|Controlled
|Material Assistance is subject to published, substantive Project controls.

|P4
|No Material Assistance
|Material Assistance is prohibited; only Incidental Assistance is permitted.
|===

=== Common classification requirements

When an adopted Provenance Profile distinguishes Incidental Assistance from
Material Assistance, the Contributor MUST classify the AGS use according to
the definitions in this Standard.

The Contributor MUST consider the complete course of assistance rather than
only the final retained text or other material. Editing, translating,
rearranging, or combining AGS output does not make Material Assistance
Incidental Assistance.

When reasonable uncertainty remains after review, the Contributor MUST treat
the use as Material Assistance unless the Project resolves the classification
under a published Project Policy.

A Project MAY classify stated forms of assistance as Material Assistance but
MUST NOT classify assistance as Incidental when it fails the definition of
Incidental Assistance in this Standard.

Incidental Assistance does not require Disclosure under ZACS unless the
selected profile or Project Policy expressly requires it.

=== P0 -- Accountable

P0 establishes the minimum provenance and human-accountability requirements
for every ZACS adoption.

For each Covered Contribution, the Contributor MUST:

. be an identifiable human;
. personally review the complete Contribution;
. understand the Contribution sufficiently to explain its purpose,
  methodology, material design decisions, operation, and validation to the
  extent applicable to the Contribution;
. understand it sufficiently to maintain or revise it to the degree reasonably
  expected for the Contributor's role;
. reasonably believe that they, or the person or organization on whose behalf
  they submit, have the right to submit it under the Project's applicable
  terms;
. disclose Known Source Material and material provenance information relevant
  to the Project's rights or obligations, unless it is already clearly
  identified in the Contribution or its preserved notices;
. not knowingly conceal Third-Party Material or its source;
. preserve required copyright, license, and attribution notices;
. accept responsibility for the contents of the Contribution regardless of the
  tools used; and
. cooperate reasonably in investigating and correcting suspected provenance,
  rights, or conformance problems.

P0 does not require Disclosure solely because an AGS was used. Any Disclosure
required by another applicable Project rule remains required.

The Project MUST provide a mechanism by which the Contributor can make the P0
Attestation required by this Standard.

If the complete Contribution cannot be personally reviewed because of its
size, format, opacity, or method of production, it does not satisfy P0 merely
because a sample, summary, manifest, or automated report was reviewed. A
Project MAY exclude objectively identified material from its adoption scope,
but it MUST NOT represent excluded material as conforming.

When multiple humans materially prepare a Contribution, each such person MUST
make the applicable Attestation for the material they prepared unless one
Contributor personally reviews the complete Contribution, satisfies every
Contributor requirement for it, and affirmatively accepts responsibility for
the complete Contribution. Organizational authorization alone does not
satisfy these conditions.

==== P0 and organizational submissions

A Contributor MAY submit on behalf of an organization. In that case, the
Contributor MUST reasonably believe they are authorized to submit the
Contribution and make the required Attestation on the organization's behalf.

The Contributor's Attestation addresses the matters assigned to the
Contributor by ZACS. It does not, by itself, establish legal authorship,
ownership, agency, or authority under applicable law.

=== P1 -- Disclosed

P1 incorporates P0 and requires disclosure of Material Assistance.

Before a Covered Contribution is accepted, the Contributor MUST disclose each
known use of Material Assistance through the Project's Disclosure Mechanism.

The Disclosure MUST identify, to the extent reasonably known:

. that Material Assistance occurred;
. the AGS product, service, model, or tool used; and
. the portions or categories of the Contribution materially affected.

When more than one AGS materially affected the Contribution, the Disclosure
MUST identify each system to the extent reasonably known. A Contributor MUST
NOT knowingly identify only an intermediary tool when doing so would conceal a
materially relevant underlying system.

The Contributor MUST update the Disclosure when they discover that it was
materially incomplete or inaccurate before acceptance. After acceptance, the
Contributor MUST notify the Project through a reasonably available channel.

The Project MUST make the Disclosure Mechanism reasonably accessible and MUST
associate the Disclosure with the Covered Contribution or its review record.

P1 does not require publication of prompts, confidential information,
credentials, personal data, or protected security information. Withholding
such material does not permit the Contributor to omit the minimum Disclosure
required by P1.

=== P2 -- Documented

P2 incorporates P0 and P1 and requires a reviewable record of Material
Assistance and the Contributor's validation.

For each disclosed use of Material Assistance, the Contributor MUST document,
to the extent applicable and reasonably known:

. the purpose for which the AGS was used;
. the nature of the generated, transformed, or materially influenced content;
. the affected files, components, sections, assets, or other material;
. the material methodology or workflow used;
. Known Source Material supplied to, identified by, or reproduced through the
  AGS;
. material warnings, citations, or provenance indications produced by the AGS;
. the human review performed; and
. the tests, comparisons, research, or other validation performed.

Documentation MUST be sufficiently specific for a reasonable reviewer to
understand the role of Material Assistance and evaluate the Contributor's
review. A statement such as "AI was used" does not satisfy P2.

P2 does not require verbatim prompt or conversation logs unless the Project
Policy expressly requires them. A summary MAY be used when it supplies the
required information without disclosing confidential information, credentials,
personal data, or protected security information.

The Contributor MUST NOT include protected information merely to satisfy P2.
When required Documentation cannot be provided safely or lawfully, the
Contributor MUST identify the omission and provide the greatest non-sensitive
description reasonably available. The Project determines whether the remaining
record is sufficient for acceptance.

The Project MUST preserve required P2 Documentation with the Contribution or
its review record for at least as long as the Project preserves the associated
contribution history, unless legal, privacy, security, or records-management
requirements require earlier removal.

Removal of protected information from a public record does not itself create
nonconformance when the Project preserves enough non-sensitive information to
support the applicable conformance claim.

=== P3 -- Controlled

P3 incorporates P0 through P2 and requires the Project to impose and publish
substantive controls on Material Assistance.

The Project Policy MUST identify:

. the categories of Contributions or Project areas in which Material
  Assistance is permitted, conditional, or prohibited;
. restrictions applicable to AGS products, services, models, tools, features,
  or modes of operation;
. requirements for handling Project-confidential, personal, security-sensitive,
  licensed, or other protected input;
. any required approval, additional review, testing, isolation, or provenance
  procedure;
. any permitted exception process; and
. the consequences and corrective process for noncompliance.

P3 requires a minimum control baseline beyond P2 Disclosure and Documentation.
The Project Policy MUST include:

. at least one use-boundary control limiting where, when, or through which
  systems Material Assistance may be used;
. at least one information-protection control governing confidential,
  personal, security-sensitive, licensed, or other protected input; and
. at least one acceptance safeguard requiring approval, additional Human
  Review, testing, isolation, provenance review, or an equivalent measure.

Examples of substantive constraints include:

* permitting only locally operated or Project-approved systems;
* prohibiting protected Project material from being supplied to an external
  system;
* prohibiting Material Assistance for security-critical or designated
  components;
* requiring approval before Material Assistance is used in designated areas;
* requiring specified provenance checks or independent review; or
* requiring additional validation for AGS-affected material.

The examples are informative. A Project MAY adopt other substantive controls
consistent with this Standard, but its controls MUST collectively satisfy the
minimum baseline above. P3 identifies Project-controlled use; it does not imply
that every P3 Project imposes identical controls or an identical degree of
risk reduction.

The Contributor MUST comply with every P3 control applicable to the Covered
Contribution. Disclosure of a violation does not cure the violation.

An exception conforms only when the Project Policy expressly permits it and
the exception satisfies the exception requirements of this Standard. The
Project MUST document the scope, justification, and approval of each exception.

=== P4 -- No Material Assistance

P4 incorporates P0 and the Common Classification Requirements. It replaces
the ordinary disclosure, documentation, and controlled-use requirements of P1
through P3 because a Contribution prepared with Material Assistance cannot
conform to P4. The corrective disclosure requirements stated in P4 remain
applicable.

A Covered Contribution MUST NOT be prepared with Material Assistance. The
Contributor MUST NOT use Material Assistance and MUST make reasonable inquiry
about material supplied by another person or organization when circumstances
provide a credible indication that Material Assistance may have been used.
AGS use in preparing the Contribution is permitted only when it satisfies the
definition of Incidental Assistance.

Disclosure or Documentation of Material Assistance does not make the
Contribution conform to P4. A Project-specific exception MUST NOT permit
Material Assistance while the Project claims P4 conformance for the affected
scope.

When actual or suspected Material Assistance is discovered, the Contributor
MUST disclose it to the Project for investigation. This corrective Disclosure
does not make the affected Contribution conforming, but it satisfies the duty
not to conceal the suspected nonconformance.

The Project MUST NOT accept a Contribution under P4 while it knows, or has an
unresolved material basis to believe, that the Contribution was prepared with
Material Assistance. The Project MAY permit a new or replacement Contribution
prepared through a process that independently satisfies P4.

P4 does not prohibit conventional non-generative tools, ordinary accessibility
tools, non-generative reference materials, or Incidental Assistance.

The name "No Material Assistance" describes the ZACS profile requirement. It
does not determine legal authorship, copyrightability, originality, or
ownership.

=== Profile comparison

|===
|Requirement |P0 |P1 |P2 |P3 |P4

|Identifiable human accountability
|Yes |Yes |Yes |Yes |Yes

|Personal review and sufficient understanding
|Yes |Yes |Yes |Yes |Yes

|Material Assistance profile treatment
|Not restricted |Disclosure |Documentation |Project-controlled |Prohibited

|Material Assistance Disclosure
|No |Yes |Yes |Yes |Corrective only

|Material Assistance Documentation
|No |No |Yes |Yes |Not applicable

|Published substantive Project controls
|No |No |No |Yes |Prohibition

|Incidental Assistance profile treatment
|Not restricted |Not restricted |Not restricted |Not restricted
|Not restricted
|===

"No" in this table means the identified requirement is not imposed by that
profile. It does not prohibit a Project from adopting a stricter compatible
requirement.
