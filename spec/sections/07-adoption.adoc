== Adoption

=== Adoption model

A Project adopts ZACS by publishing an Adoption Notice that satisfies this
section and by implementing the requirements of the identified Provenance and
Verification Profiles.

Adoption is self-declared unless a separate authorized assessment program
expressly states otherwise. Publication of an Adoption Notice does not, by
itself, establish conformance.

A Project does not need to add multiple ZACS-specific files. The Adoption
Notice, Project Policy, disclosure instructions, Verification Procedure, and
Project-specific guidance MAY appear in one reasonably accessible document.

Suitable locations may include:

* `ZACS.md`;
* `CONTRIBUTING.md`;
* a repository policy document;
* a contribution portal or issue-form policy;
* an internal policy available to Contributors in a private Project; or
* another location presented to Contributors before or during submission.

The filename does not affect conformance. Accessibility and completeness of the
published information determine whether the adoption requirements are met.

=== Required Adoption Notice contents

An Adoption Notice MUST identify:

. the Project name;
. the complete Adoption Identifier;
. a Normative Specification Reference;
. the adoption scope;
. the Effective Date;
. the location of the applicable Project Policy;
. the Disclosure Mechanism;
. the Verification Procedure;
. any Project-specific requirements;
. any exception process permitted by the selected profiles; and
. a contact or reporting mechanism for questions and suspected nonconformance.

When an item is contained in the same document as the Adoption Notice, the
notice MAY identify the applicable heading instead of another file or URL.

If the selected profile does not permit or require an exception process, the
Adoption Notice MUST state that no exceptions are permitted or identify the
applicable unconditional rule.

=== Normative Specification Reference

The Adoption Notice MUST provide a reasonably accessible reference to the
complete, unmodified Official ZACS Publication identified by its version.

The Project MUST satisfy this requirement by providing either:

* a permanent, version-specific URL published by the ZACS steward; or
* an included unmodified copy of an official release artifact.

A moving URL or reference such as `latest`, `current`, `main`, or `most recent`
MUST NOT be the normative reference. A Project MAY additionally provide such a
link for convenience when it is clearly identified as non-normative.

When a local copy is used, the Project MUST:

. identify the original ZACS version and source;
. preserve the complete specification, copyright, license, and trademark
  notices;
. avoid inserting Project-specific requirements into the specification text;
  and
. make the local copy reasonably accessible wherever the Adoption Notice is
  used.

The Project SHOULD verify a local copy against a checksum published with the
official release. Renaming an official artifact does not itself modify the
specification, but the Project MUST NOT use a filename or presentation that
misrepresents its version or official status.

A Project MAY include an officially published Markdown, AsciiDoc, HTML, plain
text, or PDF edition. A Project-generated format conversion is not an
unmodified official artifact unless the ZACS steward has designated that
converted edition as official for the identified version.

=== Standard Adoption Notice

A conforming Project MUST publish an Adoption Notice containing every item in
the following template. Formatting and explanatory language MAY change, but no
required field or assurance may be omitted or materially weakened:

[source,text]
----
ZACS Adoption Notice

Project: [PROJECT NAME]
Adoption Identifier: [EXACT ZACS ADOPTION IDENTIFIER]
Normative Specification: [PERMANENT VERSION URL OR LOCAL OFFICIAL COPY]
Scope: [COVERED CONTRIBUTIONS, REPOSITORIES, BRANCHES, OR WORKFLOWS]
Effective Date: [DATE, TIME, OR OBJECTIVELY DETERMINABLE EVENT]
Project Policy: [URL, FILE, OR HEADING]
Disclosure Mechanism: [URL, FILE, FORM, FIELD, OR OTHER MECHANISM]
Verification Procedure: [URL, FILE, OR HEADING]
Project-Specific Requirements: [LOCATION OR "NONE"]
Exceptions: [PROCESS AND LOCATION OR "NONE"]
Contact: [REPORTING OR CONTACT MECHANISM]

The [PROJECT NAME] Project adopts [ADOPTION IDENTIFIER] for the scope stated
above. Covered Contributions submitted on or after the Effective Date are
subject to the identified ZACS profiles and Project Policy.

This adoption is self-declared. It does not imply that the ZACS steward has
audited, certified, approved, sponsored, or endorsed this Project.
----

The bracketed fields MUST be replaced with Project-specific information. A
Project MUST NOT publish unresolved placeholders in an operative Adoption
Notice.

The Project MAY change formatting or integrate the fields into an existing
policy document, provided every required item remains clear and reasonably
accessible.

=== Compact Adoption Notice

A Project MAY use the following compact form when all referenced information is
available through the linked Project Policy:

[source,text]
----
The [PROJECT NAME] Project adopts [ADOPTION IDENTIFIER] for [SCOPE], effective
[EFFECTIVE DATE]. The complete, unmodified normative specification is available
at [PERMANENT VERSION URL OR LOCAL OFFICIAL COPY]. Project procedures,
Disclosure, Verification, stricter requirements, exceptions, and reporting are
published at [PROJECT POLICY LOCATION]. Adoption is self-declared and does not
imply certification or endorsement by the ZACS steward.
----

The compact form does not remove any required Adoption Notice content. Each
required item MUST be supplied either in the notice or through its identified
Project Policy.

=== Adoption scope

The scope MUST be specific enough that a reasonable Contributor can determine
whether a Contribution is covered.

A scope MAY identify:

* an entire organization or Project;
* one or more repositories;
* identified branches or release lines;
* source, documentation, artwork, data, or other material categories;
* pull requests, patches, issue forms, uploads, or other workflows;
* Contributions from identified Contributor groups; or
* another objectively determinable boundary.

A scope such as "selected Contributions" or "where applicable" is insufficient
unless the Project Policy provides an objective method for determining which
Contributions are selected or applicable.

A Project MUST state material exclusions with prominence reasonably comparable
to the adoption claim.

=== Effective Date and pending Contributions

The Effective Date MUST be stated as a calendar date, a date and time, a
release event, or another objectively determinable event.

The Adoption Notice MUST state how Contributions already under review on the
Effective Date are treated. The Project MAY:

* exclude them from the new adoption;
* require them to satisfy the new adoption before acceptance; or
* define an objectively limited transition period.

The Project MUST NOT represent Contributions as retroactively conforming unless
the applicable Attestation, provenance, and Verification requirements were
completed and the Adoption Notice expressly provides for retroactive treatment.

=== Project Policy

The Project Policy MUST provide the operational information necessary to apply
the selected profiles. As applicable, it MUST identify:

* how a Contributor makes the required Attestation;
* how and where Disclosure and Documentation are submitted;
* controls required by P3;
* how P4 classification questions are resolved;
* reviewer selection and independence requirements;
* the V2 demonstration process and accessible alternatives;
* record-preservation practices;
* correction, reconsideration, and enforcement procedures; and
* how suspected rights, provenance, or conformance concerns are reported.

A Project Policy MAY impose stricter compatible requirements. Such requirements
MUST be identified as Project-specific and MUST NOT be inserted into or
represented as part of the unmodified ZACS specification.

=== Disclosure Mechanism

The Disclosure Mechanism MUST be available to a Contributor before acceptance
and MUST permit the information required by the selected Provenance Profile to
be associated with the Covered Contribution.

A Project MAY use a pull-request template, commit trailer, issue-form field,
manifest, upload field, policy-controlled comment, or another durable
mechanism.

The Project MUST NOT require public disclosure of confidential information,
credentials, personal data, or protected security information when a protected
Project channel can preserve the required non-public record.

=== Verification Procedure

The Adoption Notice or Project Policy MUST describe how the selected
Verification Profile is performed.

For V0, it MUST identify the Attestation mechanism. For V1, it MUST
additionally identify the Human Review and reviewer-independence process. For
V2, it MUST additionally identify the demonstration method, prohibited
Contemporaneous Generative Assistance, preserved Evidence, and accessible
alternatives.

=== Multiple adoption scopes

A Project MAY adopt different profile combinations for different scopes. Each
combination MUST have its own complete Adoption Identifier, scope, Effective
Date, and applicable Project Policy.

When scopes overlap, the Project MUST expressly identify one complete,
previously adopted Adoption Identifier that governs each overlapping Covered
Contribution. A Project MUST NOT derive a new profile combination from the
overlap or leave the governing combination to a default inference.

A Project MUST NOT combine a Provenance Profile from one ZACS version with a
Verification Profile from another version.

=== Changes and upgrades

Changing an Adoption Identifier, scope, Effective Date, or requirement that
materially affects Contributor obligations requires an updated Adoption Notice.

The updated notice MUST identify:

. the prior Adoption Identifier or policy, when applicable;
. the new Adoption Identifier or material change;
. the new Effective Date; and
. the treatment of pending Contributions.

Publication of a new ZACS version does not automatically upgrade a Project.
The Project remains pinned to its adopted version until it publishes and
implements an updated Adoption Notice.

Editorial changes to contact details, links, or presentation do not require a
new Effective Date when they do not change the applicable requirements or
reduce accessibility.

=== Suspension and withdrawal

A Project MAY suspend or withdraw an adoption prospectively by publishing a
notice that identifies the affected scope and effective time.

Suspension or withdrawal does not change the historical Adoption Identifier or
the requirements that applied when a Covered Contribution was submitted. The
Project SHOULD preserve prior notices and policies when reasonably necessary to
interpret historical Contribution records.

A Project MUST NOT continue making a present-tense conformance claim for a
suspended or withdrawn scope.

=== Adoption badges and claims

A Project MAY use an official adoption badge when the badge accurately states
the complete Adoption Identifier and is used according to the applicable
trademark and badge policy.

An adoption badge MUST NOT use "certified", "approved", or equivalent wording
unless an authorized program has independently granted that status.

The absence of review by the ZACS steward MUST NOT be concealed when a claim or
badge could otherwise reasonably imply independent assessment.
