== Conformance

=== Normative language

The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT",
"SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY", and
"OPTIONAL" in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14,
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2119[RFC 2119], and
https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8174[RFC 8174], when, and only when, they
appear in all capitals as
shown here.

Lowercase uses of these words have their ordinary meanings.

=== Conformance model

ZACS defines requirements along two independent assurance dimensions:

* a Provenance Profile, identified by `P` followed by a profile number; and
* a Verification Profile, identified by `V` followed by a profile number.

A Project claiming ZACS conformance MUST adopt exactly one Provenance Profile
and exactly one Verification Profile from the same ZACS version.

The selected profiles form a single Adoption Identifier in the following
order:

`ZACS-{version}-{Provenance Profile}-{Verification Profile}`

For example:

`ZACS-0.1-draft-P2-V1`

The canonical form uses uppercase `ZACS`, `P`, and `V`, the version component
stated in the publication header, and no spaces. Adoption Identifiers are
case-sensitive and MUST be reproduced exactly in conformance claims.

Profile identifiers from different ZACS versions MUST NOT be combined in one
Adoption Identifier.

=== Profile inheritance

Profiles are cumulative within each assurance dimension. Unless a profile
expressly replaces a lower-profile requirement, adoption of a higher-numbered
profile incorporates every requirement of each lower-numbered profile in the
same dimension.

An express replacement changes only the requirement identified by the
replacing profile. All other inherited requirements remain applicable.

Requirements from one assurance dimension MUST NOT be treated as satisfying or
replacing requirements from the other dimension. A Provenance Profile does not
establish Verification conformance, and a Verification Profile does not
establish Provenance conformance.

=== Project conformance

A Project conforms to an adopted ZACS version and profile combination only if
the Project:

. publishes an Adoption Notice meeting the requirements of this Standard;
. identifies the exact ZACS version, Provenance Profile, and Verification
  Profile adopted;
. identifies the Contributions, repositories, branches, workflows, or other
  locations within the adoption scope;
. identifies the adoption's effective date;
. publishes any Project Policy necessary to apply the selected profiles;
. provides each disclosure, attestation, review, and exception mechanism
  required by the selected profiles;
. applies every applicable requirement of both selected profiles to covered
  Contributions submitted on or after the effective date; and
. does not represent a materially incomplete or modified implementation as
  conforming to the unmodified profile combination.

A Project MUST make its Adoption Notice and applicable Project Policy
reasonably accessible to prospective Contributors before or during submission.

=== Adoption scope

A Project MAY adopt ZACS for all Contributions or for a clearly identified
subset. A limited scope MUST be described with enough specificity that a
reasonable Contributor can determine whether a Contribution is covered.

A Project MUST NOT describe an entire repository or organization as conforming
when its published adoption scope covers only part of that repository or
organization, unless the limitation is stated with comparable prominence.

Material submitted before the effective date is not retroactively covered
unless the Adoption Notice expressly states otherwise and identifies how the
Project will apply the requirements to that material.

=== Project-specific requirements

A Project MAY impose requirements stricter than those required by its selected
profiles. Stricter requirements MUST be clearly identified as Project-specific
requirements and MUST NOT be presented as part of the unmodified ZACS text.

A Project-specific requirement MUST NOT remove, weaken, contradict, or create
an exception to an applicable ZACS requirement while the Project claims
conformance to the same profile combination.

If a Project modifies, omits, or weakens an applicable requirement, it MUST NOT
claim conformance to the affected profile combination. It MAY accurately state
that its policy is informed by or derived from ZACS, provided the statement
identifies the relevant modifications and does not imply conformance.

=== Exceptions

An exception is conforming only when the adopted profile expressly permits
that kind of exception and the Project follows every condition imposed on it.

A permitted exception MUST:

. be documented through the mechanism identified by the Project Policy;
. identify its scope and justification;
. be approved by a person or role authorized by the Project Policy; and
. preserve all requirements not affected by the exception.

A Project MUST NOT create an exception to an unconditional `MUST` or
`MUST NOT` requirement unless this Standard expressly authorizes the exception.

=== Contribution treatment

A covered Contribution satisfies the Project's adopted ZACS requirements only
when the Contributor and Project complete every requirement applicable to that
Contribution under both selected profiles and the Project Policy.

Acceptance or merging of a Contribution does not, by itself, establish that
the Contribution satisfied ZACS. A Project SHOULD preserve the disclosures,
attestations, approvals, and review records reasonably necessary to support its
conformance claim.

When nonconformance is discovered after acceptance, the Project SHOULD
investigate and take proportionate corrective action. Corrective action MAY
include obtaining missing disclosure, repeating review, correcting the
Contribution, reverting it, or removing affected material.

=== Conformance claims

A conformance claim MUST include the complete Adoption Identifier and MUST NOT
omit either assurance dimension.

A conforming claim MAY state:

`This Project adopts ZACS-0.1-draft-P2-V1.`

A Project MUST NOT use a higher profile number in a conformance claim unless it
satisfies that profile and every inherited requirement.

A Project MUST NOT describe adoption or self-attested conformance as
"certification", "certified", "approval", "approved", or an equivalent claim
unless an expressly authorized certification or approval program exists and
the Project has completed that program.

ZACS adoption does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, audit, or independent
verification by the ZACS steward.

=== Version pinning and upgrades

A conformance claim applies only to the exact ZACS version identified in the
Adoption Notice. Publication of a newer ZACS version does not automatically
change an existing adoption.

To adopt another version or profile combination, the Project MUST publish an
updated Adoption Notice containing the new Adoption Identifier and effective
date. The Project SHOULD identify any transition period and the treatment of
Contributions already under review.

A moving reference such as `latest`, `current`, or `most recent` MUST NOT be
used as the normative version reference in an Adoption Notice.

=== Nature and limits of conformance

ZACS conformance is based on documented Project procedures, Contributor
attestations, and the review required by the adopted profiles. It is not an
independent guarantee of authorship, provenance, legality, originality,
copyrightability, license compatibility, correctness, security, privacy, or
fitness for a particular purpose.

Conformance does not grant copyright or patent rights, replace a project
license, replace a contributor license agreement, or modify the Developer
Certificate of Origin.
