== Enforcement and Correction

=== Purpose

This section establishes minimum procedures for responding to suspected or
confirmed ZACS nonconformance. It does not prescribe a universal disciplinary
system or require a Project to accept any Contribution.

For purposes of this section, nonconformance means failure to satisfy an
applicable requirement of the adopted ZACS profiles, this Standard, or a
Project-specific requirement incorporated through the Project Policy.

The primary purposes of enforcement are to:

* prevent acceptance of unresolved nonconforming Contributions;
* correct inaccurate Attestations, Disclosures, Documentation, and records;
* identify and remediate affected material;
* protect Project participants and applicable rights;
* distinguish good-faith error from intentional concealment; and
* preserve the integrity of the Project's conformance claims.

=== Required Project procedure

The Project Policy MUST publish a correction and enforcement procedure that
identifies:

. how suspected nonconformance may be reported;
. who receives and evaluates reports;
. available interim measures;
. how the Contributor is notified and may respond;
. possible corrective and participation outcomes;
. how material decisions are recorded;
. how reconsideration may be requested; and
. how confidential, personal, security-sensitive, or legally restricted
  information is handled.

The procedure MUST be reasonably accessible to Contributors. A private Project
MAY limit access to persons authorized to participate in the covered workflow.

A Project MAY address ZACS enforcement through an existing contribution,
security, moderation, or compliance process when that process satisfies every
requirement of this section.

=== Reporting suspected nonconformance

The Project MUST provide at least one reasonably accessible reporting
mechanism. The mechanism SHOULD support non-public reports when public
disclosure could expose confidential information, personal data, security
vulnerabilities, credentials, or disputed third-party material.

A report SHOULD identify, to the extent reasonably available:

* the affected Contribution;
* the suspected requirement or Project Policy violation;
* the relevant Attestation, Disclosure, Documentation, or review record;
* the factual basis for the concern; and
* any apparent urgency or continuing risk.

A Project MUST NOT require a reporter to prove nonconformance before accepting
a report for initial assessment. A report does not, by itself, establish that
nonconformance occurred.

The Project SHOULD protect reporters and participants from retaliation for a
good-faith report or response. Knowingly false reports and deliberate abuse of
the reporting process MAY be addressed under the Project's applicable conduct
policies.

=== Initial assessment and interim measures

The Project MUST make an initial assessment sufficient to determine whether the
report is within scope, facially unsupported, requires clarification, or
warrants investigation.

While a material concern remains unresolved, the Project MUST NOT represent the
affected Contribution as having completed the applicable ZACS process.

Before acceptance, the Project MAY pause review, prevent merging, request
additional Evidence, or require correction.

After acceptance, the Project MAY apply proportionate interim measures,
including:

* labeling or isolating affected material;
* temporarily disabling distribution or use;
* restricting a release or deployment;
* reverting or quarantining the Contribution;
* limiting access to sensitive records; or
* initiating a security, legal, license, or provenance review.

An interim measure is not a final determination. It SHOULD be no broader or
longer than reasonably necessary to manage the identified risk.

=== Notice and opportunity to respond

Before making a material adverse determination, the Project MUST ordinarily
provide the Contributor with:

. notice of the material concern;
. enough non-protected information to understand and respond to it;
. a reasonable opportunity to clarify, correct, or supply Evidence; and
. notice of the possible outcome under the Project Policy.

The Project MAY delay or limit notice when reasonably necessary to protect a
person, preserve Evidence, contain a security incident, comply with law, honor
a binding confidentiality obligation, or prevent continuing harm.

When notice is delayed or limited, the Project SHOULD provide the omitted
process as soon as the reason permits. The Project MUST NOT disclose protected
information merely to satisfy this section.

Failure to respond MAY be considered using the available Evidence, but it MUST
NOT automatically be characterized as intentional concealment without an
additional reasonable basis.

=== Investigation

An investigation MUST be reasonably scoped to the suspected nonconformance and
the risk presented. The person evaluating the matter MUST consider relevant
available Evidence, including, as applicable:

* the Covered Contribution and its history;
* the Contributor's Attestation;
* required Disclosures and Documentation;
* review and Verification records;
* Project controls and approved exceptions;
* the Contributor's explanation and corrective action;
* credible provenance, license, or source indications; and
* relevant automated or human review results.

Automated detection, authorship classification, similarity analysis, or AGS
detection MUST NOT be treated as conclusive by itself. Material results MUST be
evaluated in context by an identifiable human before an adverse determination.

The investigator SHOULD avoid requesting broad prompt histories, unrelated
account data, private communications, or device access when narrower Evidence
can reasonably address the concern.

The Project SHOULD assign a person without a material conflict of interest to
make the determination. When complete independence is not reasonably available,
the Project MUST disclose or record the material conflict and apply the
reconsideration process required by this section.

=== Determinations

The Project MUST base a material determination on the available Evidence and
record one of the following or a substantively equivalent outcome:

* no nonconformance found;
* insufficient Evidence to determine;
* correctable nonconformance;
* nonconformance corrected;
* unresolved nonconformance; or
* intentional or reckless misrepresentation or concealment.

A finding of insufficient Evidence is not a finding that the Contribution
conformed. The Project determines whether the remaining uncertainty permits
acceptance under its selected profiles and other applicable policies.

A determination MUST distinguish the status of the Contribution from any
decision affecting the Contributor's participation. A nonconforming
Contribution does not automatically establish contributor misconduct.

=== Good-faith error and intentional concealment

The Project SHOULD treat prompt self-reporting, cooperation, reasonable
reliance on ambiguous guidance, and effective corrective action as indications
of good faith.

The Project MAY consider the following as evidence of greater culpability when
supported by the record:

* knowingly false Attestation or Disclosure;
* deliberate omission of required Documentation;
* concealment or falsification of Known Source Material;
* fabrication or alteration of Verification Evidence;
* prohibited Contemporaneous Generative Assistance used to defeat V2;
* repeated similar nonconformance after clear notice; or
* obstruction, retaliation, or destruction of relevant Evidence.

No single factor is automatically conclusive. The Project MUST consider the
context and available contrary Evidence before characterizing conduct as
intentional or reckless.

=== Corrective outcomes

For a correctable matter, the Project MAY require one or more of the following:

* correction or renewal of the Attestation;
* completion or correction of Disclosure or Documentation;
* additional Human Review or Verification;
* removal or replacement of affected material;
* preservation or restoration of required notices;
* new testing, provenance analysis, or license review;
* completion of a permitted exception process; or
* resubmission under a process that independently satisfies the adopted
  profiles.

Correction MUST satisfy every applicable requirement. Disclosure of a
violation does not, by itself, cure the violation.

Before acceptance, a Covered Contribution with unresolved material
nonconformance MUST NOT be accepted under the Project's ZACS adoption.

After acceptance, the Project MUST take reasonable action proportionate to the
risk and available remedies. Action MAY include correcting, reverting,
removing, replacing, isolating, or ceasing distribution of affected material.

When complete removal or correction is not reasonably possible, the Project
MUST document the known limitation and MUST NOT continue an unqualified
conformance claim for the affected Contribution.

=== Participation outcomes

The Project Policy MAY authorize participation measures such as education,
warning, additional review, temporary restriction, removal of privileges, or
exclusion from the Project.

A participation measure SHOULD be proportionate to the supported conduct,
history, risk, corrective action, and need to protect the Project and its
participants. ZACS does not require punishment for a good-faith mistake.

Nothing in this Standard requires a Project to continue reviewing, accept, or
merge a Contribution. A Project MUST NOT falsely describe an ordinary editorial
or technical rejection as a ZACS enforcement determination.

=== Urgent legal, rights, privacy, and security matters

A Project MAY take immediate action without completing the ordinary process
when reasonably necessary to address an apparent legal obligation, rights
violation, privacy exposure, credential disclosure, active security risk, or
other imminent material harm.

Immediate action MAY include restricting access, disabling distribution,
reverting a change, preserving Evidence, or referring the matter to an
appropriate legal, security, platform, or rights-holder process.

Urgent action does not determine legal liability or excuse the Project from
providing later notice and review when reasonably possible.

ZACS does not replace a statutory notice-and-takedown process, court order,
contractual obligation, law-enforcement request, or other binding legal
procedure.

=== Reconsideration

The Project MUST provide a reasonable method to request reconsideration of a
material adverse determination or participation measure.

A request MAY be limited to a stated period and MUST identify a claimed factual
error, procedural error, new Evidence, disproportionate outcome, or other
reasonable basis.

When reasonably practicable, a person who did not make the original decision
SHOULD evaluate the request. A small Project unable to provide a different
reviewer MAY reconsider using the complete record and MUST document the result.

The Project MAY decline repetitive requests that present no material new basis.

=== Records, privacy, and transparency

The Project MUST preserve enough information to identify material reports,
Evidence considered, determinations, corrective action, and reconsideration
outcomes for as long as reasonably necessary to support its conformance claims
and other applicable obligations.

The Project MUST limit collection, access, publication, and retention of
personal, confidential, or security-sensitive information to what is reasonably
necessary for the process and applicable obligations.

ZACS does not require public identification of a reporter, Contributor,
reviewer, or disciplined participant beyond what the public contribution record
and other applicable policies already require.

The Project SHOULD publish aggregate or de-identified enforcement information
when useful for improving the Standard or Project Policy and lawful to do so.

=== Effect on Project conformance

An isolated nonconformance does not automatically terminate a Project's ZACS
adoption when the Project applies its published procedure and takes reasonable
corrective action.

A Project MUST NOT claim conformance for a scope in which it knowingly and
materially fails to implement the adopted profiles or enforcement procedure.

Failure to enforce a requirement in one instance does not amend this Standard,
waive the requirement for another Contribution, or create a Project-specific
exception unless the adopted profile and Project Policy authorize that
exception.
