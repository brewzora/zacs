== Contribution Attestation

=== Purpose

The Contribution Attestation records the assurances made by the identifiable
human Contributor for a Covered Contribution. It supplies Evidence required by
V0 and inherited by every higher Verification Profile.

An Attestation is a statement by the Contributor. It is not independent proof
of the matters stated and does not make the Project a certifying body.

=== Required association

Each Attestation MUST be reliably associated with:

. the identifiable human Contributor;
. the Covered Contribution or an unambiguous set of Covered Contributions;
. the Project receiving the Contribution;
. the complete Adoption Identifier; and
. the date or objectively determinable submission event.

A Project MAY obtain one Attestation for multiple Contributions only when the
covered Contributions are unambiguously identified, governed by the same
Adoption Identifier, and submitted or authorized by the same Contributor.

=== Standard Attestation

The Project MUST obtain the following Core Attestation and applicable
Provenance Profile statement before accepting a Covered Contribution.

The Project MAY adapt formatting, field labels, pronouns, or presentation to
its submission system. It MAY incorporate the Attestation by a clear reference
to an accessible copy. A translation MUST faithfully communicate every
applicable statement, identify the language and ZACS version translated, and
state that the English text of the Official ZACS Publication controls if the
meanings conflict. The Project MUST NOT omit, combine, qualify, or alter an
applicable assurance while claiming ZACS conformance. Project-specific
assurances MUST be separately identified.

==== Identification

The Attestation record MUST identify:

* `Project`: the adopting Project;
* `Contribution`: the Contribution or covered set of Contributions;
* `Contributor`: the identifiable human making the Attestation; and
* `Adoption Identifier`: the exact ZACS version and selected profiles.

==== Core Attestation text

The Contributor MUST affirm the following statements, subject only to the
permitted presentation and translation changes above:

. I am the identifiable human Contributor responsible under ZACS for this
  Contribution, whether I submit it personally or on behalf of another person
  or organization.
. I have personally reviewed the complete Contribution.
. I understand the Contribution sufficiently to explain its purpose,
  methodology, material design decisions, operation, and validation to the
  extent applicable.
. I understand it sufficiently to maintain or revise it to the degree
  reasonably expected for my role.
. I reasonably believe that I, or the person or organization on whose behalf I
  submit, have the right to submit the Contribution under the Project's
  applicable terms.
. I have disclosed Known Source Material and material provenance information
  relevant to the Project's rights or obligations unless it is already clearly
  identified in the Contribution or its preserved notices, and I have not
  knowingly concealed Third-Party Material or its source.
. I have preserved copyright, license, attribution, and other notices required
  for material included in the Contribution.
. I have made the Disclosures and supplied the Documentation required by the
  Project's adopted ZACS profiles and Project Policy.
. I accept responsibility for the contents of the Contribution regardless of
  the tools used.
. I can explain the Contribution from my own understanding and, when required
  by the adopted Verification Profile, will do so without Contemporaneous
  Generative Assistance.
. I will cooperate reasonably in investigating and correcting suspected
  provenance, rights, or ZACS conformance problems involving the Contribution.
. I understand that this Attestation does not transfer copyright, grant a
  patent license, replace a CLA or DCO, or guarantee that the Contribution is
  lawful, original, correct, secure, or otherwise fit for a particular purpose.

=== Provenance Profile statements

The Project MUST append the statement for its selected Provenance Profile to
the Core Attestation. Because profiles are cumulative, the selected statement
incorporates the applicable lower-profile obligations.

==== P0 statement

P0 requires no additional AGS-use statement beyond the Core Attestation. The
Project MUST nevertheless make the complete P0 responsibilities reasonably
accessible to the Contributor.

==== P1 statement

For P1, the Contributor MUST additionally affirm:

* I have disclosed each known use of Material Assistance and identified the
  systems and affected portions or categories to the extent reasonably known.

==== P2 statement

For P2, the Contributor MUST additionally affirm:

* I have disclosed and documented each known use of Material Assistance,
  including its purpose, material effect, affected material, Known Source
  Material, human review, and validation to the extent required and reasonably
  known.

==== P3 statement

For P3, the Contributor MUST additionally affirm:

* I have disclosed and documented each known use of Material Assistance,
  complied with every applicable Project control, identified every approved
  exception, and have no known unapproved deviation from those controls.

A known or suspected deviation MUST be reported separately through the
Project's correction or enforcement process. The Contributor MUST NOT make the
P3 Attestation until the deviation is corrected, determined not to be a
deviation, or covered by an approved exception permitted under P3.

==== P4 statement

For P4, the Contributor MUST additionally affirm:

* To the best of my knowledge after reasonable inquiry, this Contribution was
  not prepared with Material Assistance. I did not use Material Assistance in
  preparing it, and any AGS use in its preparation satisfied the definition of
  Incidental Assistance.

Disclosure or Documentation of Material Assistance MUST NOT replace the P4
statement or cure P4 nonconformance.

=== Affirmative act

The Contributor MUST make the Attestation through an affirmative act reasonably
calculated to communicate agreement. Acceptable mechanisms MAY include:

* selecting a control that is not preselected;
* submitting a pull-request or issue-form declaration;
* applying an attributable commit trailer or sign-off that clearly incorporates
  the Attestation;
* signing an electronic or physical statement; or
* using another attributable Project-approved mechanism.

Opening an account, remaining silent, viewing the Attestation, or submitting
through a control selected by default MUST NOT constitute an Attestation.

An automated system MAY record or transmit an Attestation already authorized
by the Contributor, but it MUST NOT make, infer, or independently alter the
Attestation on the Contributor's behalf.

=== Commit and pull-request mechanisms

A Project MAY use a commit trailer such as:

[source,text]
----
ZACS-Attestation: ZACS-0.1-draft-P2-V1
----

The trailer satisfies V0 only when the Project Policy clearly states that
applying it affirmatively incorporates the complete Attestation applicable to
that Adoption Identifier and the commit is attributable to the Contributor.

A Project MAY instead use a pull-request checkbox or form field containing or
linking to the complete applicable Attestation. The control MUST NOT be
preselected.

The ZACS Attestation SHOULD remain separate from a DCO sign-off. A Project MAY
display or collect both in one workflow, but it MUST NOT modify the text of the
DCO while representing it as the unmodified DCO.

=== Changes after Attestation

When a Contribution is materially changed after Attestation, the Contributor
MUST review the changed Contribution and renew or expressly reconfirm the
Attestation before acceptance.

A Project Policy MAY define changes that do not require renewal, such as a
maintainer-applied formatting correction, only when the change does not alter
the substance, provenance classification, required Disclosure, required
Documentation, or applicable profile outcome.

When another person materially changes the Contribution, that person becomes a
Contributor for the changed material and MUST make the applicable Attestation,
unless the material is separately submitted and attested through another
covered process.

=== Correction and withdrawal

A Contributor who discovers that an Attestation was materially inaccurate or
incomplete MUST notify the Project promptly through a reasonably available
channel and provide corrected information.

The Project MUST provide a reasonably accessible method for correcting or
withdrawing an Attestation. The Project MAY preserve the prior Attestation and
correction when necessary to maintain an accurate review or compliance record.

Correction does not automatically cure nonconformance. The Project MUST apply
the review, corrective, and enforcement procedures required by the adopted
profiles and Project Policy.

=== Relationship to rights and agreements

The ZACS Attestation is not a copyright assignment, patent license, contributor
license agreement, employment agreement, confidentiality agreement, or DCO.

Any license granted with a Contribution arises from the Project's separately
applicable contribution terms, license, CLA, DCO process, or other agreement.
The Project MUST NOT describe the ZACS Attestation itself as granting rights
that it does not expressly grant.
