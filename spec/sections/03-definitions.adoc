== Definitions

For purposes of this Standard, the following definitions apply.

Adoption Identifier::
The complete identifier formed from an exact ZACS version, one Provenance
Profile, and one Verification Profile. For example,
`ZACS-0.1-draft-P2-V1`.

Adoption Notice::
The Project's published statement adopting an exact ZACS version and profile
combination. An Adoption Notice identifies the Adoption Identifier, adoption
scope, Effective Date, and location of the applicable Project Policy.

Attestation::
A statement or set of statements made by an identifiable human Contributor to
provide the assurances required by this Standard, an adopted profile, or the
Project Policy.

Automated Generative System (AGS)::
A system that generates or substantially transforms expressive or functional
material in response to prompts, context, examples, instructions, or materially
equivalent input. AGS includes generative artificial-intelligence systems,
large language models, agentic development tools, and materially equivalent
systems, regardless of how they are marketed or implemented.
+
A tool is not an AGS merely because it is automated. A conventional compiler,
formatter, calculator, search facility, or deterministic transformation tool
is not an AGS unless the relevant function generates or substantially
transforms material in a manner described above.

Automated Review::
Evaluation of a Contribution performed by an automated system. Automated
Review may produce useful Evidence but is not Human Review and does not, by
itself, establish a Contributor's understanding.

Contribution::
Code, tests, documentation, data, designs, artwork, audio, configuration, or
other material offered to a Project for incorporation, distribution,
publication, operation, or other Project use.

Contributor::
The identifiable human to whom Contributor requirements apply and who submits
or authorizes submission of a Contribution, whether personally or on behalf of
another person or organization. An AGS is not a Contributor for purposes of
this Standard.

Contemporaneous Generative Assistance::
Use of an AGS while preparing or providing a response in a Verification
Procedure intended to assess the Contributor's own understanding. The term
does not include non-generative reference materials, conventional
non-generative translation tools, or an accessibility tool used solely to
change modality, presentation, navigation, or communication. The exclusion
for accessibility tools does not apply when the tool generates substantive
answers, explanations, reasoning, or other content responsive to the
Verification Procedure.

Covered Contribution::
A Contribution within the scope and effective period stated in a Project's
Adoption Notice.

Disclosure::
A reasonably accessible statement identifying AGS use or another fact required
to be revealed by an adopted profile. Disclosure communicates that the use or
fact occurred; it does not necessarily provide the detail required by
Documentation.

Disclosure Mechanism::
The location and method designated by a Project for making a required
Disclosure. Examples may include a pull-request field, commit trailer, issue
form, manifest, or other Project-controlled record.

Documentation::
Recorded information sufficient to describe a matter to the extent required by
an adopted profile or Project Policy. Documentation of AGS use may include the
system used, affected material, methodology, source context, review, and
validation, as applicable.

Effective Date::
The date, time, or objectively determinable event after which the Adoption
Notice applies to Contributions within its stated scope.

Evidence::
Information or a record used to support an Attestation, review result, or
conformance determination. Evidence does not become independent proof merely
because a Project preserves or relies on it.

Human Review::
Review performed by an identifiable human who examines the complete material
within the required scope and applies the judgment required by the adopted
profile. Automated Review may assist Human Review but cannot replace it unless
this Standard expressly provides otherwise.

Incidental Assistance::
AGS use for a mechanical, clerical, informational, or advisory purpose when no
generated or substantially transformed expressive or functional material is
retained in the Contribution and the use does not materially determine the
Contribution's design, methodology, implementation, behavior, correctness,
security, privacy, licensing, provenance, or maintainability.
+
Assistance is not Incidental Assistance merely because the retained output is
short, edited, or combined with human-created material.

Known Source Material::
Source Material that the Contributor knows, or reasonably should know, is
Third-Party Material. Knowledge may arise from direct identification, supplied
context, recognizable content, a citation, a model response, or another
credible indication of origin.

Material Assistance::
AGS use that generates or substantially transforms material retained in a
Contribution, or that materially determines the Contribution's design,
methodology, implementation, behavior, correctness, security, privacy,
licensing, provenance, or maintainability.
+
When AGS use does not satisfy every condition of Incidental Assistance, it MUST
be treated as Material Assistance unless another adopted-profile category
expressly applies.

Material, Materially, and Substantive::
Significant enough that the matter could reasonably affect a Contribution's
provenance, licensing, design, methodology, implementation, behavior,
correctness, security, privacy, maintainability, acceptance, or applicable
conformance outcome. The determination considers the circumstances and the
information known or reasonably available when it is made. Related effects
MUST be considered together and MUST NOT be treated as immaterial solely
because each effect would be minor in isolation.

Normative Specification Reference::
The reasonably accessible reference in an Adoption Notice to the complete,
unmodified Official ZACS Publication identified by the adopted version.

Official ZACS Publication::
A complete, unmodified ZACS publication released by the ZACS steward through
an official repository or publication channel. A modified or independently
maintained derivative is not an Official ZACS Publication.

Project::
The repository, organization, product, service, working group, or other
collaborative body adopting this Standard for an identified scope of
Contributions.

Project Policy::
The Project's published procedures for implementing its selected ZACS version
and profiles. A Project Policy may identify disclosure mechanisms, required
records, review procedures, permitted exceptions, enforcement processes, and
stricter Project-specific requirements.

Provenance Profile::
One of the `P` profiles defined by this Standard. A Provenance Profile governs
human accountability, AGS disclosure and documentation, source-material
handling, and restrictions on AGS assistance.

Retained Output::
Material generated or substantially transformed by an AGS that remains in a
Contribution after the Contributor's review, including material retained in
edited, translated, rearranged, or combined form.

Source Material::
Preexisting material used as input, context, an example, a basis for
transformation, or a source for material included in a Contribution.

Third-Party Material::
Material in which rights are held or claimed by a person or entity other than
the Contributor or the person or organization on whose behalf the Contribution
is submitted. The term does not determine whether the material is protected or
whether a particular use is legally permitted.

This Standard::
The complete, unmodified document identified by the ZACS Identifier stated in
the publication header.

Verification::
A process required by a Verification Profile to evaluate an Attestation,
review activity, Contributor understanding, or other conformance matter.
Verification supplies Evidence; it does not guarantee that the verified matter
is true, complete, lawful, original, correct, or secure.

Verification Procedure::
The documented method used by a Project to perform Verification, including any
required reviewer, question, demonstration, record, timing, or acceptance
criterion.

Verification Profile::
One of the `V` profiles defined by this Standard. A Verification Profile
governs how a Project checks or obtains Evidence of Contributor attestations,
review, and understanding.

ZACS::
The family of Zero Ambiguity Contribution Standard publications maintained by
the ZACS steward. A reference to ZACS without an exact version and profiles
does not identify a complete conformance target.

ZACS Identifier::
The identifier of an exact Official ZACS Publication, excluding the profiles
selected by an adopting Project. It MUST be reproduced exactly as stated in
the publication header. For example, `zacs-0.1-draft`.
