== Scope

=== Purpose

ZACS governs contribution provenance, disclosure of automated generative
assistance, human accountability, and verification of a Contributor's review
and understanding.

It provides Projects with a versioned framework for selecting and communicating
requirements through independent Provenance and Verification Profiles.

ZACS applies to Contributions within the scope identified by a conforming
Project Adoption Notice. It may be adopted by public or private Projects and
by open-source, source-available, or closed-source Projects.

=== Covered material

A Contribution may include any material offered to a Project for incorporation,
distribution, publication, operation, or other Project use, including:

* source code and generated code;
* tests, examples, and build or deployment material;
* documentation and other text;
* configuration and structured data;
* datasets and database content;
* designs, specifications, and diagrams;
* artwork, images, models, animation, audio, and video; and
* patches, revisions, or transformations of existing material.

The form or submission mechanism does not determine whether material is a
Contribution. Covered material may be submitted through a pull request, patch,
commit, issue, form, upload, messaging system, or another mechanism identified
by the Project.

A Project MAY limit its adoption to identified categories of material or
submission mechanisms, subject to the adoption-scope requirements of this
Standard.

=== Covered automated assistance

ZACS applies when an Automated Generative System is used in preparing a covered
Contribution and the use falls within a category regulated by the adopted
Provenance or Verification Profile.

Covered use may include an Automated Generative System used to:

* generate material retained in a Contribution;
* substantially transform existing material;
* translate material between programming, data, markup, or natural languages;
* propose or materially determine design, architecture, behavior, or
  methodology;
* identify or reproduce third-party material;
* analyze, test, review, or validate a Contribution when the adopted profile
  regulates that activity; or
* assist a Contributor while responding to a verification procedure.

Automated assistance may be covered even when no generated output is copied
verbatim into the Contribution. The applicable profile determines whether the
assistance is Incidental Assistance, Material Assistance, prohibited
assistance, or another defined category.

Ordinary use of an Automated Generative System outside the preparation, review,
submission, or verification of a covered Contribution is outside the scope of
ZACS unless the Project Policy expressly brings that use within scope.

=== Covered participants and actions

ZACS assigns requirements to identifiable human Contributors and adopting
Projects as specified by each profile.

ZACS may govern:

* Contributor review and attestation;
* disclosure and documentation of automated assistance;
* identification and handling of Known Source Material;
* Project-provided submission and disclosure mechanisms;
* human or automated review procedures;
* demonstrations of Contributor understanding;
* preservation of conformance records; and
* investigation and correction of suspected nonconformance.

For purposes of ZACS, an Attestation MUST be made by an identifiable human. An
Automated Generative System cannot make an Attestation or satisfy a requirement
assigned to a Contributor.

A human MAY submit a Contribution on behalf of an organization. The
organization may own or control rights in the Contribution, but the
identifiable human making the Attestation remains responsible for satisfying
the Contributor requirements of the adopted profiles.

ZACS does not determine the legal authorship or ownership of a Contribution.

=== Matters outside the scope of ZACS

ZACS does not:

* determine whether any person or system is an author under applicable law;
* determine copyrightability, ownership, originality, infringement, fair use,
  or license compatibility;
* grant, transfer, or assign copyright, patent, trademark, trade-secret, or
  other rights;
* certify the contents, sources, or legal status of model training data;
* prove that generated or transformed material is free of third-party rights;
* guarantee that a Contribution is lawful, correct, secure, private,
  maintainable, accessible, or fit for a particular purpose;
* approve or prohibit a particular product, provider, model, or tool except as
  expressly stated in a Project-specific policy;
* regulate development activity that is not within the Project's published
  adoption scope; or
* establish professional, employment, procurement, or regulatory compliance
  outside the requirements expressly stated by this Standard.

An Attestation, disclosure, review, or verification record is evidence supplied
or produced through a Project process. It is not independent proof of
provenance, legality, originality, correctness, or security.

=== Relationship to other terms

ZACS is designed to complement, not replace:

* the license governing the Project or Contribution;
* a contributor license agreement;
* the unmodified Developer Certificate of Origin;
* employment or contractor agreements;
* confidentiality and security requirements;
* third-party attribution and notice obligations; and
* applicable laws and regulations.

Adoption of ZACS does not alter any separate agreement or legal obligation.
When another applicable term imposes a stricter compatible requirement, the
Contributor and Project remain responsible for satisfying that requirement.

If compliance with ZACS would conflict with applicable law or a binding legal
obligation, the affected party SHOULD notify the Project through an appropriate
channel and MUST NOT make a conformance claim that conceals the unresolved
conflict.

=== Project extensions

A Project MAY extend the subject matter covered by its Project Policy or impose
stricter compatible requirements. Extensions MUST be distinguished from the
unmodified requirements of this Standard and MUST comply with the conformance
rules for Project-specific requirements.
