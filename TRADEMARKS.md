## 3. Uses allowed without separate permission

You may, in a truthful and non-misleading manner:

- refer to ZACS for commentary, criticism, education, news, comparison, or
  compatibility;
- link to an official ZACS publication;
- state that a project has adopted a particular official ZACS version and
  profile when it has satisfied the applicable adoption requirements;
- reproduce the word marks as reasonably necessary to identify the standard;

- describe a modified work as "based on ZACS" or "derived from ZACS" when the
  modification and lack of official status are made clear.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, certification, approval, affiliation,
or endorsement that does not exist.

## 4. Official publications

Only complete, unmodified publications released through the official ZACS
repository or website may be described as an "official ZACS specification" or
"official ZACS release" or as the definitive text of a ZACS version.

A modified specification must:

1. be clearly identified as modified;
2. identify the official ZACS version from which it was derived, when known;
3. use a name that does not create confusion with an official ZACS
   publication; and
4. avoid any claim of Steward approval or endorsement.

A suitable notice is:

> This document is derived from the Zero Ambiguity Contribution Standard
> (ZACS), version [VERSION]. It has been independently modified and is not an
> official ZACS publication or endorsed by Brewzora Studios.

## 5. Project adoption

A project may truthfully state that it has adopted an official ZACS version
and profile. Adoption alone does not mean that the Steward audited, certified,
approved, sponsored, or endorsed the project.

Unless the Steward establishes a formal certification program, projects must
not use statements such as "ZACS Certified," "certified by ZACS," or
"Brewzora approved."

## 6. Logos and badges

The unmodified ZACS logo may be used to identify or link to the official ZACS
project if the use does not imply endorsement.

An official adoption badge may be used only:

- for the version and profile actually adopted;
- in the form supplied by the Steward;
- under the published badge and adoption requirements; and
- without implying independent verification or certification.

The ZACS logo or an adoption badge must not be incorporated into another
party's trademark, organization name, product name, domain name, or application
icon without written permission.

- merchandise the marks other than for incidental, noncommercial community

- use the marks in a way not otherwise permitted by this policy and likely to
  imply sponsorship, certification, approval, or affiliation.
